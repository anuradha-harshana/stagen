1.  Executive  Summary  
This  proposal  outlines  the  development  of  a  modern  cloud-based  Construction  Progress  &  
Customer
 
Experience
 
Platform
 
designed
 
specifically
 
for
 
Australian
 
residential
 
home
 
builders.
 
The  platform  aims  to  improve  communication,  transparency,  customer  satisfaction,  operational  
efficiency,
 
and
 
post-handover
 
warranty
 
management
 
throughout
 
the
 
home
 
construction
 
lifecycle.
 
Unlike  traditional  construction  management  systems  that  primarily  focus  on  internal  project  
operations,
 
this
 
platform
 
is
 
designed
 
around
 
the
 
customer
 
journey
 
while
 
also
 
providing
 
supervisors,
 
trades,
 
and
 
builders

The  system  will  provide:  
●  Real-time  construction  progress  tracking  ●  Timeline-based  activity  updates  ●  Photo  and  document  management  ●  Customer  notifications  ●  Invoice  and  billing  notifications  ●  Warranty  and  defect  tracking  ●  FAQ  and  AI-powered  customer  assistance  ●  Multi-tenant  builder  management  ●  Auto-scalable  cloud  infrastructure

3.  Problem  Statement  
Residential  builders  and  customers  commonly  experience:  
●  Limited  visibility  into  project  progress  ●  Repetitive  customer  calls  and  emails  ●  Poor  update  frequency  ●  Unclear  construction  timelines  ●  Disconnected  communication  channels  ●  Difficulty  managing  warranty  issues  post-handover  ●  Lack  of  centralized  document  access  ●  Delays  caused  by  inefficient  communication  
Most  existing  construction  management  systems  are  focused  on  internal  operations  and  are  
overly
 
complex
 
for
 
residential
 
customer
 
engagement.
 
This  platform  aims  to  solve  these  problems  through  a  simple,  visual,  customer-focused  
experience.

9.  Multi-Tenant  SaaS  Design  
The  platform  will  support  multiple  builder  organizations  using  a  shared  SaaS  platform  
architecture.
 
Each  organization  will  operate  as  a  separate  tenant  with  isolated:  
●  Projects  ●  Users  ●  Documents  ●  Notifications  ●  FAQ  content  ●  Warranty  records  ●  Billing  data  ●  Branding  configuration  
The  architecture  will  support  future  enterprise  scaling.  
Multi-Tenant  Requirements  
●  Every  company  is  represented  as  a  tenant/organization  ●  All  key  data  tables  include  tenant_id or  organization_id  ●  Backend  APIs  enforce  tenant-level  isolation  ●  Users  can  only  access  data  within  their  assigned  organization  ●  Platform  administrators  can  manage  all  tenants  ●  Audit  logs  record  tenant,  user,  action,  and  timestamps  
White-Label  Branding  
Each  tenant/company  can  configure:  
●  Company  logo  
--- PAGE 13 ---
●  Brand  colours  ●  Customer  portal  theme  ●  Branded  emails  ●  Branded  PDFs  and  reports  ●  Company-specific  support  details  ●  Optional  custom  subdomain  support  
Locale  &  Translation  Support  
The  platform  will  support  localisation  and  multilingual  capabilities.  
Locale  Features  
●  Default  locale  per  company  ●  Locale  preference  per  user  ●  Time  zone  support  ●  Locale-aware  date,  time,  and  currency  formatting  ●  Australian  English  as  default  initial  locale  
Translation  Features  
●  Translation  key-based  UI  design  ●  Multi-language  support  for  UI  labels  ●  Translated  notification  templates  ●  Translated  email  templates  ●  Translated  FAQ  content  ●  Fallback  language  support  ●  Future  support  for  additional  languages
